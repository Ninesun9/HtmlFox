HtmlFox Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

HtmlFox (“the app”) is a tool for opening, previewing, editing, and exporting HTML files on your device. We designed it to work entirely on-device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. HtmlFox has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your files or any usage information.

Data stored on your device

  • Your documents. When you open an HTML file, a copy is saved inside the app’s private storage so you can reopen it. These files never leave your device and are not accessible to the developer.
  • Recent files list. The names of recently opened documents are stored locally on your device only, to populate the “Recent” list.

You can remove this data at any time by deleting recent items in the app or by uninstalling the app, which erases all of its stored data.

Network access

HtmlFox does not send your data anywhere. The app itself makes no network requests. However, an HTML document you open may reference external resources (such as images, fonts, or scripts hosted on the internet). When the document is rendered, your device may load those resources directly from their servers, exactly as a web browser would. This behavior is controlled entirely by the content of the file you choose to open. Tapping a web link in a document opens it in your system browser.

Children’s privacy

HtmlFox does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

HtmlFox 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 3 日

HtmlFox（“本应用”）是一款在你的设备上打开、预览、编辑并导出 HTML 文件的工具。我们的设计目标是让它完全在本地运行。本政策说明本应用 会以及不会如何处理你的信息。

我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何个人数据。HtmlFox 没有账号、没有数据分析、 没有广告、也没有任何追踪。开发者不会接收、存储或访问你的文件或任何使用信息。

存储在你设备上的数据

  • 你的文档。当你打开一个 HTML 文件时，应用会在其私有存储区 保存一份副本，以便你再次打开。这些文件不会离开你的设备，开发者也无法访问。
  • 最近打开列表。最近打开文档的名称仅保存在你的设备本地， 用于显示“最近打开”列表。

你可以随时在应用内删除最近项目，或卸载应用来清除以上所有数据——卸载会 一并抹除应用存储的全部数据。

网络访问

HtmlFox 不会把你的数据发送到任何地方，应用本身不发起网络请求。但你打开的 HTML 文档可能引用外部资源（例如托管在互联网上的图片、字体或脚本）。在渲染 该文档时，你的设备可能会像网页浏览器一样，直接从这些资源所在的服务器加载 它们。此行为完全取决于你所打开文件的内容。点击文档中的网页链接将在系统 浏览器中打开。

儿童隐私

HtmlFox 不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，更新后的版本将发布在本页面，并标注新的“最后更新”日期。
